How PartnerAI works · No black box, no replacement of judgment

Expert-led. AI-assisted. Decision-ready analytics that synthesize and trace large datasets, made easy with PartnerAI.

Most teams collect far more data than they can act on. Reports arrive after the decision window has closed, and no one can trace a finding back to who said it. PartnerAI turns scattered inputs into decision-ready evidence while there is still time to use it.

01
Real-time ingestion and synthesis

Primary and secondary data lands, and findings update live across the team. Everyone sees what is emerging while there is still time to act on it. Secondary sources join the same evidence base, indexed to exact page references.

02
Transparency and traceability

Every finding links to its source: who said it, where it came from, which interview or document. Follow the thread from final report back to original statement, and forward again.

03
Experts lead, AI supports

The people who conduct the interviews and write the reports review and react to incoming data in real time. AI never leads. Accuracy is checked by the people accountable for it.

04
Language and localization

Models capture rich detail in local languages; bilingual speakers verify the record. Data quality widens across geographies instead of narrowing to English.

05
Quality and bias audits

Bias and coverage checks run while the research runs. Every flag carries a corrective action and a named owner. Section 1 shows two still open.

06
Secure, purpose-limited use

Project data serves its assigned project only, under a written protocol. No client data trains models.

In the field · Real programs, real results

Where PartnerAI has already delivered

PartnerAI runs on MarketEdge engagements today. These are real programs where it helped teams move from data to decisions.

UK–Ghana JET Programme
Palladium · FCDO-funded

Ghana’s 24H+ industrialization agenda proposed a network of regional textile and garment parks. Before committing public money, the government needed to know which sites could attract private investment, and the timeline was tight. MarketEdge led the pre-feasibility and comparative site analysis with Beyond Borders Development Group, using PartnerAI to run analysis in real time and check findings as they emerged, and to structure stakeholder input across government, manufacturers, and developers. We built an investor and tenant pipeline, and stood up AI agents that let government counterparts explore and rehearse how to engage investors in the textile and garment sector. The work delivered a transparent site ranking, early anchor-tenant targets, and a capital-ready roadmap to mobilize private participation.

FCDO COAST — SEA Coastal Leaders
DAI · Funded by UK Aid

Ocean decisions are made globally but lived locally. MarketEdge ran a six-month cohort that prepared eight community leaders from six Southeast Asian countries for the 2026 Our Ocean Conference. We combined practical workshops, one-to-one mentorship, and the OOC Dashboard powered by PartnerAI, which leaders used to research the organizations they wanted to meet and prepare for each session. They arrived able to identify the right counterparts, sharpen their messages, and carry community priorities into the room.

WWF Mentorship Program
Bolivia and Myanmar country teams

WWF asked MarketEdge to help its Bolivia and Myanmar teams apply political economy analysis where power dynamics and competing interests decide conservation outcomes. We designed a hands-on mentorship program across all three service areas, built around real challenges: forest fires in Bolivia and conflict dynamics in Myanmar. Teams used PartnerAI stakeholder personas to rehearse live negotiations, then defined WWF’s role and built action plans with one-to-one coaching.

USAID/Bangladesh PSLA
Private Sector Landscape Assessment

USAID/Bangladesh needed to understand how to engage the private sector in social impact programming. MarketEdge conducted and transcribed 93 key informant interviews, then used PartnerAI to analyze them into decision-ready findings at a fraction of the usual cost and time. Drawing on a curated resource library, PartnerAI surfaced 22 shared value partnership models with clear next steps, and the Mission moved quickly from insight to investment decisions.

World Vision Australia — BASIN programme
Mekong market and value chain analysis

MarketEdge ran a market and value chain analysis across the Mekong for World Vision Australia under the BASIN programme, using PartnerAI to synthesize market data into intervention-ready recommendations. The analysis moved past description to specific concept notes the team could take forward directly.
