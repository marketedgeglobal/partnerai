How PartnerAI works · No black box, no replacement of judgment

Expert-led. AI-assisted. Traceable end to end.

MarketEdge built PartnerAI for its own engagements and runs it on its own projects today. Clients get the version that survived our fieldwork.

01
Real-time ingestion and synthesis

Primary and secondary data lands, and findings update live across the team. Everyone sees what is emerging while there is still time to act on it. Secondary sources join the same evidence base, indexed to exact page references.

02
Transparency and traceability

Every finding links to its source: who said it, where it came from, which interview or document. Follow the thread from final report back to original statement, and forward again.

03
Experts lead, AI supports

The people who conduct the interviews and write the reports review and react to incoming data in real time. AI never leads. Accuracy is checked by the people accountable for it.

04
Language and localization

Models capture rich detail in local languages; bilingual speakers verify the record. Data quality widens across geographies instead of narrowing to English.

05
Quality and bias audits

Bias and coverage checks run while the research runs. Every flag carries a corrective action and a named owner. Section 1 shows two still open.

06
Secure, purpose-limited use

Project data serves its assigned project only, under a written protocol. No client data trains models.
